# Deathnote Forever

**Write down the players who killed you, share your lists with your guild and party, get notified when somebody finds your target and get the revenge you deserve.**

That rogue who ganked you at the flight master. The warrior who camped your corpse for ten minutes. The hunter who pulled you into a mob of elites. You will meet them again, and Deathnote Forever makes sure you know the moment you do.

---

## How it works

Add a player to your Deathnote with a short reason. From then on, whenever that player shows up near you, Deathnote Forever warns you:

- **On-screen warning** in raid warning style, with the name and your reason
- **Warning sound**, so you notice even mid-fight
- **Skull marker** above their nameplate
- **Tooltip line** "Deathnote: reason" when you mouse over them

Detection uses nameplates, your target and mouseover. Deathnote Forever also raises the enemy nameplate distance, so you spot your enemies as early as the game allows.

## Who killed you?

Died and didn't catch the name? Open the **Recent** tab. Deathnote Forever remembers the enemy players who took part in your death or attacked you, with their class, the zone and the time. Click **+** to add one to your list with the reason already filled in, or **X** to dismiss it. Battlegrounds and arenas are skipped.

## Share with your guild and party

Deathnote Forever users in your guild and party automatically exchange their lists. One player's enemy becomes everyone's enemy.

- When a guild or party member spots someone from a list, you get a chat message with the **zone and coordinates**. If it happens in your zone, you also get an on-screen notice.
- When you spot someone from your list while in a party or raid, Deathnote Forever posts "Name spotted nearby" in group chat, so members without the addon know too. When several of you spot the same player, only one reports it. Turn it off in the **Settings** tab.
- Type **/deathnote way** to set a map waypoint to the latest sighting and go settle the score.
- Only guild and party members can add entries to your lists. Strangers can't.

## The list

Open it with **/deathnote** or the minimap button.

- **Tabs:** Mine, Party, Guild, All and Recent. All is split into sections, so you always know where an entry came from. Party and Guild never repeat players already on your own list.
- **Faction and class:** pick them from a list for players you added by hand. They're also filled in automatically the first time you see the player.
- **Class colours:** names are coloured by class at a glance.
- **Last seen:** each entry remembers when and where the player was last spotted. Click the pin to set a waypoint there.
- **Edit reasons:** click a reason to change it.
- **Hide shared entries:** hide entries from others that you don't care about, for you only. Bring them back any time with **Unhide**.
- **Settings:** switch the group chat announcement on or off.
- **Test alert:** see and hear the warning before it matters.

## Commands

- `/deathnote` or `/dnf`: open or close the list
- `/deathnote add`: add your current target
- `/deathnote add Name reason`: add a player by name
- `/deathnote del Name`: remove a player (or your target)
- `/deathnote way`: waypoint to the latest sighting
